Common questions

Santa isn't answering me

The app needs the microphone. Go to Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone and switch on Talking Santa. Also check the phone isn't on silent with the volume all the way down, and that no other app is using the microphone.

How do I unlock the other friends?

Tap any friend with a padlock, or the pink "more friends" bar. A grown-up answers a quick sum, then buys the unlock. It's one payment, not a subscription, and it unlocks every friend and every world.

I paid, but the friends are locked again

Open the unlock page and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with. If Family Sharing is on, family members can restore it too.

Where are my child's recordings?

Tap the pink gift button to open My Gifts. The newest 25 are kept there, and tapping the star keeps one forever.

How do I send a video to Grandma?

In My Gifts, tap Send on a recording. A grown-up answers a quick sum, the app makes a short video, then you can share it however you like.

How do I delete the recordings?

Open Grown-ups (the gear button on the friends board) and choose to delete all recordings. Deleting the app deletes them too.

Can I turn the sound effects off?

Yes. In Grown-ups, switch off Sound effects. The friends will still talk back.

I'd like a refund

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and choose the purchase.
