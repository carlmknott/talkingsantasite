Just talk, and Santa says it back in his big jolly voice. Tickle him, poke him, meet his friends and send a video to Grandma.

Hello, I'm Carl

Years ago I was the lead developer on the original Talking Santa and Friends. It was featured on the App Store and downloaded over 250,000 times. Kids loved hearing Santa say their words back in his big jolly voice.

This Christmas I've rebuilt it from scratch, with a fresh, colourful look, new friends to meet and lots of little surprises. Made for little ones, about 3 to 8.

What's inside

  • Just talk. Santa listens, then says it back in his voice. No buttons to hold.
  • Seven friends, each with their own voice, tricks and snowy world: Santa, the Reindeer, the Christmas Tree, the Snowman, the Elf, the Gingerbread Man and the Penguin.
  • Little surprises: tickles, giggles, snores, snow, parties and dancing.
  • Gifts to send: turn anything your child says into a short video for Grandma.
  • Free to try: Santa is free. One payment unlocks every friend. No subscription.
  • Calm and safe: no ads, no accounts, no tracking. Your child's voice stays on your device.
