Privacy policy

Talking Santa & Friends collects nothing. Here's what that means.

Listening

The app listens through the microphone so the friends can say back what your child says. The sound is handled on the device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Recordings

Recordings stay on your device. The newest 25, and any your child stars, are kept in My Gifts so they can be played again. A grown-up can delete them all at any time from the Grown-ups page, and deleting the app deletes them too.

Sharing a video

Nothing is sent anywhere unless a grown-up chooses to. After answering a sum, a grown-up can make a video of a recording and share it, for example in a message. That video is made on the device and goes only where the grown-up sends it.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no adverts, no tracking, no accounts and no analytics. The app does not collect, store or share any information about you or your child, and we have no servers that could receive it.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see payment details or who bought what.

Children

The app is made for children. Because it collects no personal information, there is nothing for us to keep, share or delete. Links, purchases and sharing all sit behind a quick sum for grown-ups.

Changes

If this ever changes, we'll update this page and the privacy page inside the app before the change goes live.
